Privacy policy

Nothing about you on our servers

Little Browser doesn't collect personal data. There's no account, no telemetry and no analytics, and the browser sends nothing about you or your browsing to us.

Effective 21 November 2026. This policy covers the Little Browser app on every platform and the littlebrowser.app website. Little Browser is made by the Little Browser Team.

What stays on your device

Your history, bookmarks, tabs, spaces, passwords, cookies, downloads and settings are stored on your device only. We have no copy and no way to read them. Deleting them in the browser, or uninstalling it, removes them.

What we collect

Nothing. The browser has no usage statistics, crash uploads, field trials or sign-in, and it doesn't send identifiers to us. We don't sell, rent or share data, because we don't have any.

What the browser connects to

Apart from the websites you visit, the browser contacts these services directly, not through us. Each handles the request under its own privacy policy.

  • The search engine you choose. There's no default: you pick one on first run, and your searches go to it. Search suggestions are off, so nothing is sent while you type.
  • Google Safe Browsing, to warn you about dangerous sites. The browser keeps a local list of hash prefixes and checks addresses against it on your device, and asks Google for list updates every so often. Only when a prefix matches does it send that short prefix to Google, never the address. The lists need an agreement with Google that isn't in place yet, so until then Google turns the update requests down and the browser has no lists to check.
  • The ad blocker's filter lists: EasyList and EasyPrivacy from easylist.to, uBlock filters from GitHub Pages and Peter Lowe's list from pgl.yoyo.org, downloaded every four days without cookies, along with the files a list includes. Those servers see your IP address and the browser's version and operating system (its user agent), as with any download; no cookies or identifiers are sent. Which sites you allow ads on stays on your device.
  • The Chrome Web Store, only if you use extensions. You install them from its pages, or pick a password manager on first run, which the browser fetches from the store after you approve it. While extensions are installed, the browser checks the store for updates every few hours, sending each extension's id, version, install date and whether it's turned on, but no usage pings or identifiers. The store's pages can see which extensions you have installed, as in Chrome. Extensions you install have their own privacy policies.

App stores and downloads

If you install Little Browser from an app store, the store's own privacy policy covers that. Downloads from downloads.littlebrowser.app are served by Amazon CloudFront, which sees your IP address to deliver the file. To count how often each release is downloaded, CloudFront records only the time of each request, the file, whether it was delivered and how many bytes were sent: no IP address, browser details or identifier. We add these up into the totals shown on the download page and delete the records after a week.

On a Mac, the browser checks downloads.littlebrowser.app for a new version every four hours. The check sends only the browser's version and that it runs on macOS; no identifier or system details. Updates are downloaded from a folder of their own there, so the download counts can tell new downloads from updates.

This website

littlebrowser.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. The site is served by Amazon CloudFront, and we keep no access logs.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Children

Little Browser is a general-purpose browser, not designed for children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you think we hold something, write to us and we'll check and answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. The project is open source, so every change is also visible in its history.

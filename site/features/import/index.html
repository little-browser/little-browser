Features / Import from Arc, Chrome and others

Import from Arc, Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari

Bring your bookmarks, history, search shortcuts, addresses and open tabs. From Arc, your spaces and pinned tabs come along too.

The import dialog opens on first run, and later from the command bar ("Import from another browser"), the app menu or Settings. It lists only the browsers it finds on your Mac.

Where from

  • Arc, with its spaces, pinned tabs and profiles.
  • Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium, Opera and Vivaldi, with their profiles.
  • Firefox, with its profiles.
  • Safari, when Little Browser has Full Disk Access to read its bookmarks. Otherwise export from Safari and choose the export file.
  • A bookmarks file (HTML), which every browser can export.

Questions

Can I import from Arc?

Yes. Its spaces, pinned tabs, open tabs, bookmarks, history and profiles come across, and your extensions reinstall from the Chrome Web Store.

Does importing bring my passwords?

No. Passwords and payment cards never come along. A password manager extension handles passwords, and each of them can import yours.

Can I import from Safari?

Yes. Either give Little Browser Full Disk Access, so it can read Safari’s bookmarks, or export your data from Safari and choose the export file.

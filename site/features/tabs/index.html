Pinned tabs, tab names and auto-archive

Pinned tabs that stay, names you choose, and an archive that keeps today’s list short.

Every space has two kinds of tabs: pinned tabs, which are its permanent set, and today's tabs, which are everything else.

Pinned tabs

  • Closing puts them to sleep. Close a pinned tab and it keeps its place, unloads its page and fades. Click it and the page loads again.
  • They remember a link. A pinned tab goes back to the page it was pinned on, even after you've wandered off it, and even after a restart. Use Use Current Page as Link or Edit Link… to change it.
  • Pin with a drag or a key. Drag a tab across "+ New Tab", press ⌘ D, or use the right-click menu.

Names

Right-click any tab and choose Rename Tab…. Your name replaces the page's title in the sidebar and the command bar, and the command bar finds the tab by its name and by the page's title. Names stay through restarts, space switches and sleep.

Auto-archive

Today's tabs you haven't used for a while are closed and moved to the archive, which the command bar can search. That keeps the sidebar short and memory low. Choose 12 hours, 24 hours (the default), 7 days, 30 days or never. Pinned tabs are never archived.

Also in the sidebar

  • Split view, Chromium's own: two tabs side by side in one row.
  • A speaker icon on tabs that are playing sound; click it to mute.
  • Ctrl Tab cycles tabs in the order the sidebar shows them.

Questions

What happens when I close a pinned tab?

It goes to sleep: it keeps its place, its page unloads and it fades. Clicking it loads its page again. Removing a pinned tab takes a second step, so you can’t lose one by accident.

Can I rename a tab?

Yes. Right-click it and choose Rename Tab. The name replaces the page title in the sidebar and the command bar, and it is kept across restarts.

Does Little Browser close my old tabs?

Only today’s tabs, and only if you leave auto-archive on. Tabs you haven’t used for 24 hours by default move to an archive that the command bar can search. Pinned tabs are never archived.

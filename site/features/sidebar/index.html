Features / Vertical tab sidebar

A vertical tab sidebar for your browser

Tabs and spaces sit in one place beside the page. Hide it and the page fills the window, with the sidebar a push of the pointer away.

Little Browser puts your tabs in a vertical sidebar, built on Chromium's own vertical tabs. The toolbar is at the top of the sidebar: back, forward, reload, extensions, downloads and the menu on one row beside the window buttons, and the address under them. Nothing sits above the page, and there's no bookmarks bar and no badges.

What's in the sidebar

  • The space header. The icon and name of the current space, tinted with the space's colour. A small lock badge shows when the space runs in its own profile.
  • Pinned tabs. Rows with an icon and a title, not tiles, kept per space. See pinned tabs.
  • Today's tabs. Everything else, newest first, with tab groups among them.
  • The footer. Dots to switch spaces, and a ＋ menu for a new space, tab, split view or tab group.

More page when you want it

  • Hide it with the toggle in the title row, or ⌘ S. Nothing of the sidebar stays on screen.
  • Slide it in. Rest the pointer on the window's left edge and the sidebar slides in over the page, then slides away when the pointer leaves.
  • Resize it by dragging its edge, whether it's shown or slid in.

Prefer tabs on top?

The "Tabs on top" command gives you Chromium's usual horizontal tab strip. The command bar, Peek and the shortcuts all work the same, and a sideways swipe on the tab row still switches space.

Small details

  • The page is inset with rounded corners, so the sidebar feels like part of the window.
  • A thin line along the top of the page shows how far a page has loaded, and never runs backwards.
  • Light and dark follow your system. A space's colour is a gentle tint, never a solid block.

Questions

Does Little Browser have vertical tabs?

Yes. Tabs live in a sidebar on the left, built on Chromium’s own vertical tabs. You can switch to a horizontal tab strip with the Tabs on top command.

How do I hide the sidebar?

Press ⌘S or use the toggle in the sidebar’s title row. Resting the pointer on the left edge of the window slides it in over the page, and it slides away again when the pointer leaves.

Can I make the sidebar wider?

Yes. Drag its edge, from 312 pixels wide, just room for its row of buttons, up to 600.
